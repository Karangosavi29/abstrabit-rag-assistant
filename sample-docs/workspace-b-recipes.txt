Grandma's Weeknight Dal:
- 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp cumin seeds
- Simmer lentils in 3 cups water for 20 minutes, then temper with onion,
  garlic, and spices fried in ghee.
